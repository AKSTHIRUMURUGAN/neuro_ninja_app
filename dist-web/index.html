How to Install (3 Steps)

  1. Tap Download Universal APK above. If Chrome shows "File might be harmful", tap Download anyway (normal for beta APKs outside Play Store).
  2. Open the downloaded .apk file and tap Settings → Allow from this source, then tap Install.
  3. Open NeuroNinja → Settings → Offline Voice Packs to download openWakeWord (3.5 MB) and train "Hi Ninja" with your mic.

Recommended Permissions for Testers

  • Microphone & Notifications: Required for hands-free "Hi Ninja" wake-word.
  • Unrestricted Battery: Tap Allow Unrestricted Battery in Voice Settings so background listening stays active.
